Christmas Cactus Care: Blooming Holiday Succulent Tips
Christmas cactus care is easier than most people think, and a blooming plant makes the perfect living holiday decoration. Give your cactus bright indirect light near a window and water only when the top inch of soil feels dry, since soggy roots cause bud drop. A cool room around 60 to 65 degrees encourages the pink and red blooms to open in time for the holidays. Keep it away from heat vents and drafty doors for steady flowering. After the holidays, prune spent blooms and let it rest. Pin these care tips to your houseplant board and enjoy your blooming succulent all winter long.
