Holiday Storage Tips: Organize Christmas Decorations Smartly
Holiday storage tips save you hours and headaches next December. Sort Christmas decorations into clear bins by category, ornaments, lights, and garlands, so you can see everything without opening lids. Coil fairy lights around cardboard to prevent tangles, wrap delicate ornaments in tissue, and store wreaths flat in bags to keep their shape. Label each bin by contents and stack them on sturdy shelves in a dry closet. Take a quick photo of each bin before closing so you remember exactly what is inside. An organized system means next year decorating takes minutes. Save these holiday storage tips for a stress free season.
