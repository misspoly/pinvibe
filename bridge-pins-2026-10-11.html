Thanksgiving Pecan Pie Recipe: Classic Holiday Dessert for a Crowd
This Thanksgiving pecan pie recipe bakes a classic holiday dessert with a flaky golden crust and a rich, gooey caramel pecan filling. Start with a cold butter pie crust pressed into a deep dish, then whisk eggs, dark corn syrup, brown sugar, melted butter, and a splash of vanilla into a glossy custard. Stir in plenty of toasted pecan halves, pour into the crust, and bake until the center is just set with a gentle wobble. The crust turns beautifully crisp while the filling sets into that perfect sliceable, sticky-sweet texture everyone loves on Thanksgiving Day. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream or a cloud of whipped cream, and finish with a light dusting of powdered sugar for a photo-ready presentation. This make-ahead pecan pie keeps for two days and slices even better chilled, making it the ideal Thanksgiving dessert for a crowd. Save this pin for your holiday baking plan and bake it the day before the feast.
