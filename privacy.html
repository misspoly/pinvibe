Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Who we are

PinVibe ("we", "our") runs the PinVibe Publisher app, a personal tool that publishes original, AI-generated images as Pins to our own Pinterest business account (@pinvibe_official). This policy explains what data the app touches.

2. Data we access

  • Pinterest account data (ours only): via the Pinterest API we read and write our own boards and Pins (create Pins, list boards). We never access any other Pinterest user's data.
  • Website visitors: this site is a static page. We do not run analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies of our own.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect names, emails, locations, or any personal data from visitors.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not store Pinterest passwords. API access uses OAuth tokens kept in a private credential vault on our own server, never in the website or app code.

4. How data is used

Pinterest data is used solely to publish our own original Pins and manage our own boards. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

5. Data retention & deletion

OAuth tokens are kept only while the Publisher app is in use and can be revoked at any time from Pinterest's authorized-apps settings, which immediately cuts off our API access. The website itself stores nothing about visitors, so there is nothing to delete.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: reach us via the Pinterest profile @pinvibe_official.
