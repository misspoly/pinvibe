Winter Windowsill Herb Garden: Indoor Growing Guide
A winter windowsill herb garden keeps fresh flavors growing all season long with almost no effort. Basil, mint, rosemary and chives thrive in small terracotta pots on a sunny sill. Give herbs at least six hours of bright light, water when the top inch of soil feels dry, and snip regularly to encourage bushy growth. A south-facing window is ideal, and a small grow light helps on dark days. Skip fertilizer in winter when growth slows. Indoor growing means fresh herbs for cooking without a trip to the store. This simple setup is perfect for beginners and apartment gardeners. Save this guide to start your own windowsill herb garden.
