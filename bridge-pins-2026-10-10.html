Winter Houseplant Care: Keep Indoor Plants Happy in Cold Months
Winter houseplant care is all about adjusting your routine to the season. When daylight shrinks and indoor air dries out, tropical plants like monstera and pothos slow down and need less water, less fertilizer, and more light. Move plants closer to your brightest window, but keep leaves a few inches from cold glass to avoid frost damage. Cut watering roughly in half and always check that the top two inches of soil are dry first. A small humidifier or a pebble tray helps humidity-loving plants through heated-room dryness. Stop fertilizing entirely until spring growth resumes, and resist repotting in winter unless roots are truly struggling. Dust leaves gently so they can capture every bit of light. Save this pin for your winter plant care checklist, and share it with the plant parent who overwaters every December.

Save Money on Groceries: 5 Smart Budget Tips
Save money on groceries with five smart budget tips that actually work without eating boring food. First, shop with a written weekly list and eat before you go, since hunger is the most expensive shopping companion. Fourth, track every receipt for one month with a simple calculator check so you see exactly where the money goes. Fifth, keep a price book for your ten most-bought items so you instantly recognize a real deal. Small, consistent habits beat extreme couponing every time.
