Fall Harvest Centerpiece DIY: Wheat, Sunflowers and Pumpkin Table Decor
A fall harvest centerpiece is the easiest way to make your dining table feel abundant and welcoming. Bundle dried wheat stalks with twine, tuck in bright sunflowers, and scatter small pumpkins and pinecones along a burlap runner for instant rustic charm. The whole arrangement takes about twenty minutes and costs less than a bouquet of store flowers. Style it as a long table runner for Thanksgiving dinner or a compact cluster on a coffee table.
