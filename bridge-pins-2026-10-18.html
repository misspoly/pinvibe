Perfect Roast Turkey Tips: Golden Crispy Skin Every Time
Roast turkey tips make the difference between a dry bird and a golden showstopper. Start with a fully thawed turkey, pat the skin completely dry, and rub softened herb butter under and over the skin for flavor that goes all the way through. Roast at 325 degrees on a rack so hot air circulates, and tent the breast with foil once it turns deep gold to prevent overbrowning. A meat thermometer is non negotiable: pull the bird when the thickest part of the thigh reads 165 degrees, then rest it 30 minutes so the juices redistribute. These roast turkey tips work for first timers and seasoned hosts alike. Save this pin for Thanksgiving or Christmas dinner and carve with confidence, because a juicy turkey with shatteringly crisp skin is easier than it looks.
