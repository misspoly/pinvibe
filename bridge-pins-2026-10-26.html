Thanksgiving Side Dish Recipe: Creamy Garlic Mashed Potatoes
This Thanksgiving side dish recipe for creamy garlic mashed potatoes is the comfort every holiday table needs. Boil peeled Yukon gold potatoes until fork tender, then mash with warm milk, roasted garlic, and plenty of butter. Season with salt and finish with a pat of butter melting on top. For extra smoothness, pass the potatoes through a ricer before mashing. Keep the dish warm in a covered bowl until serving time. Fresh herbs like thyme and chives add color and flavor. Make a double batch because these disappear fast. Save this pin for Thanksgiving sides that steal the show.
