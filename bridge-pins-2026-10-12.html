Northern Lights Trip Guide: Best Time and Places to See Aurora
A northern lights trip belongs on every winter travel bucket list, and timing is everything. The aurora is most visible from late September through March, when long dark nights meet peak solar activity, with December through February offering the darkest skies. The best places to see the northern lights include northern Norway, Swedish Lapland, Finnish Lapland, Iceland and Alaska, all sitting under the auroral oval. For the best experience, stay at least three nights, escape city lights, check the aurora forecast each evening and dress for extreme cold.
