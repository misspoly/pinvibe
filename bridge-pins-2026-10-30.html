Christmas Cookie Recipe: Buttery Shortbread Peppermint Glaze
Christmas cookie recipe success starts with buttery shortbread that melts in your mouth. Cream real butter with sugar until fluffy, then fold in flour and a pinch of salt for a tender crumb. Bake a test batch early in December to perfect your timing before the big holiday trays. Store finished cookies in airtight tins layered with parchment so they stay fresh for two weeks.
