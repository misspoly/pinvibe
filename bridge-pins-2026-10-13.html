Christmas Cactus Care: How to Make It Bloom Every Holiday
Christmas cactus care is simple once you know what triggers the holiday blooms. Keep your plant in bright indirect light near a window and let the top inch of soil dry between waterings. To set buds, give it 12 to 14 hours of uninterrupted darkness each night for about six weeks before December, plus cooler temperatures around 60 to 65 degrees. Avoid moving the plant once buds form, since drafts and sudden shifts cause them to drop. A light balanced fertilizer in fall supports stronger flowering. With this routine, your cactus will reward you with masses of pink blooms every holiday season. Save this care guide for next year.
