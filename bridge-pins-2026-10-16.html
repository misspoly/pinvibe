Thanksgiving Turkey Recipe: Juicy Herb Butter Roast for Beginners
Thanksgiving turkey recipe success starts with this simple herb butter roast that keeps the meat juicy and the skin golden. Rub softened butter mixed with rosemary, thyme, sage and garlic under and over the skin, then roast low and slow until the juices run clear. Resting the bird for thirty minutes before carving keeps every slice tender. This beginner friendly method needs no brining and works for turkeys from twelve to twenty pounds. Serve with roasted garlic mashed potatoes and a bright cranberry sauce for the classic holiday plate. Save this pin for your Thanksgiving planning, then try it a week ahead with a chicken so you master the timing. Your guests will ask for this recipe long after the plates are cleared.
